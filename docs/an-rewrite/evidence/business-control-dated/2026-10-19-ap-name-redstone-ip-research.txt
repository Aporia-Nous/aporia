4) Copyright (high level, ordinary English)
- Copyright protects original expression (game code, art, text, specific works), not a monopoly on an ordinary English word like “redstone” by itself.
- Trademark (and brand association / unfair competition theories) is what can restrict product naming in commerce when consumers may be confused or when a mark is used as a brand identifier.
- Using Minecraft textures/logos/assets or presenting a product as official Minecraft tooling would engage copyright + trademark + guidelines — separate from bare word use.
